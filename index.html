About the lab

The Smart Mobility Systems and Operations Lab is led by Dr. Lakshay, Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at IIT (BHU) Varanasi. Our work sits at the intersection of operations research, service analytics, and statistics — we build mathematical models that help public institutions make better decisions when their data is messy, incomplete, or uncertain, and we turn those models into simple, usable digital tools.

Most of our recent work is in public transport planning. We've partnered with major Indian transport organisations — including APSRTC, DTC, and BMTC — to modernise scheduling and operations. One outcome of this work is a schedule-automation and optimisation toolkit that was tested and delivered to the Bangalore Metropolitan Transport Corporation (BMTC); because it runs on data agencies already collect, it's ready to be adopted by other Road Transport Corporations at no extra cost.

Beyond transportation, we apply the same modelling toolkit to the circular economy and healthcare logistics — including an ongoing project on building a sustainable, low-waste blood supply chain. Across all of this, our goal is the same: intelligent, well-engineered systems that help public institutions operate smarter and more effectively.

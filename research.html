Research

Our research applies operations research, applied mathematical modelling, and data analytics to real problems in public systems — mostly transportation, but increasingly healthcare logistics and the circular economy too.

01

Public transportation planning

We design scheduling and operations tools that transit agencies can actually deploy on the data they already collect.

Before optimisation — bunched, unpredictable arrivals
optimised ▾
After optimisation — evenly spaced, reliable service
Line

Bus scheduling & timetabling

Network-aware automated bus scheduling to reduce overlap, cut operating cost, and improve punctuality — deployed with BMTC and DTC.

Line

Crew & fleet optimisation

Crew rostering and fleet assignment models that minimise overtime cost while meeting service-level requirements.

Line

Service analytics dashboards

Travel-time and passenger-demand prediction dashboards built directly on GTFS feeds and GPS pings, e.g. the DAKSH platform for DTC Delhi.

Line

Accessibility & network equity

Accessibility indicators based on travel time and walking thresholds, used to inform equitable network-planning decisions (e.g. Rajkot).

02

Electric & sustainable mobility

As agencies electrify their fleets, we help them plan the infrastructure and schedules that make the transition financially viable.

Line

Charging infrastructure planning

Optimisation frameworks for integrated charging-facility planning and priority-based charger allocation, including rural battery-electric bus systems.

Line

Charger & battery scheduling

Electric bus charger scheduling that accounts for peak load and battery degradation costs under mixed diesel/electric operation.

03

Disaster resilience & crowd safety

Earlier work in the lab focused on evacuation planning and safe crowd flow — a thread that continues into current railway-resilience research.

Line

Mass-evacuation planning

Robust and operating strategies for bus-based evacuation, and pedestrian control measures for mass gatherings.

Line

Resilient railway networks

Optimal allocation and strategic placement of railroad accident-relief equipment using simulation-based decision tools.

04

Sustainable & last-mile logistics

We extend the same optimisation toolkit beyond transit — into healthcare supply chains, e-commerce logistics, and maritime freight.

Line

Sustainable blood supply chain

Reverse-logistics models to minimise waste in blood supply chains through optimised redistribution.

Line

Last-mile & parcel logistics

Optimal location and capacity planning for parcel lockers, drone-based last-mile delivery, and sustainable last-mile logistics models.
